Netherite repairs - netherite scraps or diamond blocks to repair instead of netherite ingots
Elytra nerfs - no more boosting, no unbreaking or mending
Minecarts - experimental minecarts enabled by default, with speed set to 20
Minecart rails - crafted with nuggets instead of bars
Unstackables - can be stacked up to four times

Tools - gold tools more usable, netherite more durable but slower, diamond little less durable
    tool progression goes wood, stone, copper, iron, gold, diamond, netherite
Deaths - works hand in hand with persistency, gear stays on respawn but gets fully broken, has to be repaired.
    the rest of the items go into a grave at the death location. if died in a dangerous spot:
